About me
Driven by a childhood
curiosity of asking "why."

Born in Bologna in the late '80s, I've spent the past 14 years building an international career across Europe at the crossroads of marcom, innovation and technology — from large corporations to early-stage startups in SaaS, food retail, consumer tech and deep tech.

For the past five years I've focused on the deep tech field, collaborating with research institutions, founders, government bodies and industry ecosystems to improve positioning, visibility and stakeholder alignment. I've simplified complex topics like quantum computing and superconducting through thought leadership, feature writing and multi-channel content strategies.

"The whole is greater than the sum of its parts." — Aristotle

At heart I'm a collaborative, pragmatic person who thrives in cross-functional environments. After more than a decade of adventures across Europe, Brussels has become my fifth home — and the place where innovation, tech policy and strategy intersect in the most dynamic way.
