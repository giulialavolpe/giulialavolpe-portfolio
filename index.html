About me
Driven by a childhood
curiosity of asking "why."

I was born in Bologna, Italy, in the late '80s and over the past 14 years my journey has taken me across Europe, where I've been crafting an international career at the crossroads of marcom, innovation and technology.

From large corporations to early-stage startups at various stages of growth, I've worked across industries — SaaS, food retail, consumer tech, and deep tech — designing brand strategies, building content engines, shaping PR initiatives, and delivering high-level events where strategy, production, publishing, community, and analytics align to drive measurable impact.

I've spent the past five years exploring the deep tech field, focusing on both digital and offline communications within the quantum technology sector. I've collaborated with international research institutions, founders, government bodies, policymakers and industry ecosystems to improve positioning, visibility, and stakeholder alignment across Europe.

"The whole is greater than the sum of its parts." — Aristotle

In environments filled with complex jargon, I aim to create clarity. I've broken down intricate topics like quantum computing and superconducting, written think pieces on growth scaling, investing and human capital, and developed social media, SEO, content marketing, and tone of voice strategies from the ground up — all with the goal of crafting content that resonates, tells a story, and is truly human.

After more than a decade of professional adventures across Europe, Brussels has become my fifth home-away-from-home, and the place where innovation, tech policy and strategy intersect in the most dynamic way.
